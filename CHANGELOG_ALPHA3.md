# CarDSP v0.1.0-alpha.3

Alpha.3 focuses on audio-output visibility, wired-route compatibility, and privacy clarity.

## Added

- Dedicated **Audio Output** panel on the Player tab.
- Active output type display.
- Detailed AVAudioSession route display.
- Current sample-rate display.
- DSP engine running/stopped status.
- Manual **Refresh Audio Route** button.
- Human-readable route labels for:
  - Lightning-to-AUX / wired headphones
  - USB-C-to-AUX
  - USB audio interfaces/adapters
  - Wired line-out / AUX
  - Bluetooth A2DP
  - Bluetooth HFP
  - AirPlay
  - iPhone speaker / receiver

## Audio routing

CarDSP now explicitly supports and reports iOS-selected wired and wireless output routes. The app does not force Bluetooth; iOS remains responsible for selecting the physical output device. This allows app-owned audio to use compatible Lightning-to-AUX, USB-C-to-AUX, USB audio, line-out, Bluetooth, and AirPlay routes.

This does not bypass iOS restrictions on accessing or processing audio generated by other apps.

## Privacy

The iOS privacy usage descriptions were expanded to explain exactly why CarDSP requests Apple Music, microphone, and Bluetooth access.

CarDSP is intended to avoid user tracking and data collection:

- No advertising SDKs are intentionally included.
- No analytics/telemetry system is intentionally included.
- No App Tracking Transparency prompt is requested because the app is not designed to track users across apps or websites.
- Apple Music access is for music authorization/playback features.
- Microphone access is for user-requested RTA/acoustic measurement features.
- Bluetooth access is for audio routing and compatibility, not location or behavioral tracking.
- DSP configuration/preset data may be stored locally for app functionality.
